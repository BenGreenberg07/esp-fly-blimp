Do this on the ground with motors off: physically aim the nose at the Target X/Y point, then click 🎯 Zero heading. It measures the fixed offset between the rigid body's reported yaw and the real nose, and applies it to the streamed heading, the on-screen arrow and the heading error together. Needs tracking, and at least 0.25 m from the target.
Orbits the manual Target X/Y (center, orange ring) at this radius and altitude. Lookahead is true pure pursuit: the aim point is where a circle of this radius around the blimp cuts the ring, taking the forward one — so the goal is always exactly this far away, however far off the ring it has drifted. Off-ring the aim point sits across the ring, so it steers back on by itself. Too small is dangerous: bearing to a near point is noisy and flips ~180° if the craft drifts past it (measured: below 0.3 m the heading error p90 is 152°, versus 35° at 1.2–2.0 m). Bigger = smoother, corners cut wider. Start around 1.0 m. Set the center in Manual X/Y, then press GO to goal.
